WASD driving

Drive your robot with W/A/S/D on the same page, or tap the on-screen pad. Configure wheels, ports, reverse and speed exactly like the desktop app.

Open the controller →
Before you connect:
  • Use Chrome, Edge or Safari 17+ on a Mac — Web Bluetooth needs them.
  • Keep the SPIKE hub turned on and within a few metres.
  • Press SCAN and pick your Hub in the browser popup (that popup is the scan list).
  • First drive with W/A/S/D, SPACE = handbrake, ↑/↓ = speed.

Nothing you do in the browser touches your Mac — no files, no permissions. It just talks to the Hub.
